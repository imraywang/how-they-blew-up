# The Little Richeys (@TheLittleRicheys, YouTube): Dubbing nearly every video: 11M subscribers from 49 uploads

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-06. https://blewup.co/cases/the-little-richeys/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

A spin-off of a big family channel, with 1.5M views on its very first video. Two things pushed it to 3.5 billion views: high-concept challenges you get in one line, and multilingual dubbing on almost every upload.

## The play

Ask one question per video that fits in a sentence ($1 vs $500, can they find it, can they sneak it in), play it out with the whole family on camera, then dub it into dozens of languages and publish worldwide.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The premise formula and the dubbing can be copied as is. What is hard to copy is the head start from the parent channel and the production load of seven people filming abroad. It suits a family or small team that already has some audience and several people willing to be on camera.

- What it takes: A few on-camera people with fixed personas, premises that fit in one sentence, and the willingness to dub every video into many languages.
- Startup cost: Low at first: small tabletop experiments are enough to start. The later videos with eight-figure medians rely on real locations and expensive props, which cost noticeably more.
- Time to a verdict: About ten videos. By this report’s stop-loss, if the first 10 sit around 1M views the premise is right; if they are far below that, change the premise first.
- Window: The format itself is not new, and we have no data on how crowded it is. This channel stopped after August, so its own slot is open for now.

## At a glance

- What it is: Challenge shorts from a family of seven: cheap vs expensive, can they do it, can they sneak it in.
- Who watches: Families and kids worldwide; almost every video is dubbed into dozens of languages.
- How it earns: Only Shorts ad revenue is visible on the channel.
- How it broke out: A parent channel gave it a head start, then high-concept challenges and multilingual dubbing scaled it worldwide.

## KPI

- Subscribers: 11M (Registered 2025-03-25, 49 videos) [Fact]
- Median views, first 10 videos: 1.18M (The first video alone had 1.55M) [Fact]
- Video 13: 1.40B (40% of all channel views) [Fact]
- Est. ad revenue to date: $71K–353K (at $0.02–0.10 per 1,000 views) [Estimate]

## Key findings

1. The premise fits in one line: The biggest hits are titled as the question itself: the impossible puzzle, sneaking into the parade, $1 vs $500 water. You know what you are waiting for without watching the first three seconds.
2. Almost every video is dubbed into dozens of languages: 40 of 49 videos carry 10 or more dubbed audio tracks. Those 40 account for 99.7% of views, with a 17M median; the 9 undubbed ones have a 1.24M median.
3. It was not built from zero: This is a sub-channel of The Richey Family, and its first video got 1.55M views. What you can copy is the premise and the dubbing, not the starting point.
