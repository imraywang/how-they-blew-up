# Mau Baron (@maubaron, X): An app founder left X for 7 months, came back with revenue

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-08. https://blewup.co/cases/maubaron/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

Mau says nobody cared about his first four months, so he stopped and built a Christian app. He returned posting its revenue, replying about 25 times a day; a “Complete Guide” article reached 1.97M views. Today he has 21.4K.

## The play

Build something that earns first; come back to X and post the numbers; reply all day; then write one long guide with the result in the title.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

What you can copy is the order: build until there's a number, post the number, reply all day, then write one long guide. What you can't copy is the business behind the screenshots. Fits a founder with real revenue to show.

- What it takes: A product that earns, and enough months of it to write a guide.
- Startup cost: Your time; the money goes into the product, not X.
- Time to a verdict: Judge it two weeks after you come back with numbers: his first two weeks had a median of 23,415.
- Window: App revenue posts are everywhere on X; the detail and the consistency of your numbers are what set you apart.

## Key findings

1. Same pace, different result: Before the pause he posted about one main post a day and stayed near 100 followers, he says. Back with revenue to show, his first two weeks had a median of 23,415 views.
2. One article did the most: His Complete Guide article reached 1.97M views and 17,675 bookmarks. A week later he wrote that he had gained 3,000 followers that week.
3. His recap is off on two points: "Posting daily" was about every other day. "$20K a month when I came back" was about $15K by his own November post. The pause and the article's timing check out.
4. Replies faded, and the app ended: Replies fell from 967 in January 2026 to 55 in September. In October he posted that Prayer Lock was terminated; his bio still says $100K a month.
