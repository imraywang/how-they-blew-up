## The play

Reply every day to stay visible; when the count stalls, switch to one list format you can repeat, tag everyone in it, and sell your service before the follower number arrives.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

What you can copy is the list format: one shape, repeated daily, crediting the people in it. Also the order: sell first, count later. What you can't copy is his starting point, years on X and 9.7K followers. Fits a marketer or builder who already has a skill to sell.

- What it takes: A skill people pay for, and a fresh list topic every day for two weeks.
- Startup cost: Time, plus whatever AI tools you use to draft; he says he spent $200 a month on AI subscriptions before he earned anything.
- Time to a verdict: Judge a format after ten posts: his lists beat his old median from the first one.
- Window: Grok bots are new on X right now; a list format ties you to whatever is new, so expect to move on to the next tool.

## Key findings

1. Replies kept him seen, not growing: Two runs of 200+ replies a day, then about 86 a day for four months. His own counts went from 9,700 (Jun 18) to 9,829 (Oct 21).
2. One list format turned it: Before Oct 25 his sample median was 489 views. His 11 "10 grok bots" lists have a median of 8,055. He posted "10,000" eight days after the first one.
3. Income came before followers: He says he signed clients in August, made $6,000+ in September and $10,000 a month in October, all before he hit 10K.
4. His recaps mostly add up: The reply challenges and his income posts are consistent. "Over 180 followers" in September is not: his own counts that month barely moved.
