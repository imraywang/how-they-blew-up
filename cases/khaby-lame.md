# Khaby Lame (@khaby.lame, TikTok): One silent shrug: 100M followers in 17 months

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/khaby-lame/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

He says he lost his job in Italy's lockdown, then posted two sketches a day for a year to few viewers. Answering other people's life hacks by stitch took his median from 335K to 20.5M, and made him TikTok's most-followed person.

## The play

Reply to a video millions have already seen: stitch someone’s over-complicated life hack, do it the obvious way, and end on a blank stare and open palms. No words, so it works in every country.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The growth path has two halves you can learn from: a year of posting twice a day with no real hit, then a format that answered other people’s viral videos without a word. The scale that followed (TikTok’s promotion, a manager, global brands) is not something to copy.

- What it takes: A face that carries a joke without words, and a year of patience.
- Startup cost: A phone and a bedroom at the start; a manager and a company later.
- Time to a verdict: About two a day for the first year; the format arrived in month 12.
- Window: The silent reply is taken. The transferable part is reacting to what is already spreading, in a way that needs no language.

## At a glance

- What it is: Silent reactions to over-complicated life hacks.
- Who watches: Global; many children, by his account.
- How it earns: Brand deals through his company; $9.9M–20M a year by press estimates.
- How it broke out: Stitch replies from late 2020; the gesture from March 2021; 100M by August.

## KPI

## Key findings

1. A year at a few hundred thousand: 722 videos before the gesture, about two a day, had a median of 335K (lifetime views, so an upper bound). He had 50K followers after two months and 1M after eleven.
2. The reply, not the sketch: From the first gesture video on March 15, 2021 the median is 20.5M, 61 times higher. 56 of his videos passed 100M views; the first, on March 27, 2021, was a stitch.
3. Then a plateau: Followers have been at 160–163M since 2024. Posting slowed to about one a week, and the median since 2024 is 14.2M.
