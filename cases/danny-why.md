# Danny Why (@danny_why, YouTube): A faceless pro on camera: 6.4K to 102K in 5 months

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/danny-why/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

By his own account he has made YouTube since 2009, including faceless channels with millions of subscribers. His breakout-era videos, AI faceless-channel money tutorials by their titles, are nearly all deleted. Today: 222K.

## The play

Talk to camera about the thing your viewers are anxious about right now (views stalling, getting monetized, a new AI tool), show your own analytics as proof, and attach every video to the news of the week: “… Just Changed YouTube Forever”.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A rare record of an experienced faceless creator going on camera and staying flat for nine months. The breakout itself is not visible: the videos that took him from 6.4K to about 80K were AI money-niche tutorials he later deleted. What remains copyable is the current format: small-channel anxieties and AI-tool news, with his analytics as proof.

- What it takes: Enough YouTube experience to explain the algorithm with your own numbers, and comfort talking to camera.
- Startup cost: A camera, screen recording and an AI dubbing setup; a full day per title and thumbnail.
- Time to a verdict: About five 10–15 minute videos a month.
- Window: Very crowded, and tied to tool news that changes weekly.

## At a glance

- What it is: Advice for small YouTube channels and AI tools for creators, on camera.
- Who watches: People trying to get a channel monetized (our reading of the titles); dubbed into up to 20 other languages.
- How it earns: Ads (over $8K a month, he says), sponsors and a $29-a-month community.
- How it broke out: Autumn 2025, on AI faceless-niche videos that are now deleted.

## KPI

- Subscribers: 222K (6.4K at the end of August 2025) [Fact]
- 6.4K → 102K: ~150 days (Sep 2025 → Jan 2026, month ends) [Derived]
- Videos from the turn still public: 0 (Oct 28, 2024 – Dec 20, 2025 is empty) [Fact]
- Ad revenue, one month: $8K+ (his own figure, mid-2026) [Self-reported]

## Key findings

1. Experience didn’t carry over: From October 2024 to August 2025 the channel went from 901 to 6,390 subscribers, with April and May 2025 slightly negative. He says almost every video stopped at about 2,000 views, despite years of running faceless channels.
2. Then 6.4K to 102K, on videos that are gone: September 2025 added 11.7K subscribers, October 24.4K, November 18.9K, December 21.4K. None of the videos from those months is public now; six whose titles survive are AI faceless-niche money tutorials, and they show as unavailable.
3. Rebuilt on small-channel pain and AI news: The public channel restarts on December 21, 2025. Six videos to the end of January have a 105K median; from March, 48 videos at a 44K median, with AI-tool spikes (1.73M on Claude Code in May, +41K subscribers in June).
