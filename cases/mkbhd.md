# Marques Brownlee (@mkbhd, YouTube): Started at 15 on a laptop webcam: 1M in five years

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/mkbhd/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

He posted 218 videos in six months and had 74 subscribers at video 100. By his own account he worked alone through high school and college, from screen tutorials to headphones to phones; the first hire came in 2017.

## The play

Review the gear you actually own, as soon as it’s new, every week for years. Start with screen-recorded how-tos while you learn the camera, move to the products people search for (headphones, then phones), and keep the same plain format until it becomes yours.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The opposite of a one-video breakout: five years of reviews and tutorials, made alone around school, each year’s median several times the last. The copyable part is the early habit (review what you own, post often, move toward what people search for); the timing and the later studio are not.

- What it takes: Patience for years of small numbers, and the discipline to post weekly around school or work.
- Startup cost: A laptop webcam at the start. He bought better cameras as the channel paid for them.
- Time to a verdict: Several a day in early 2009, then one to three a week for five years.
- Window: Tech review is crowded; the method (own the gear, be first, explain plainly) still works in narrower niches.

## At a glance

- What it is: Tech reviews and explainers; in the early years, tutorials and gadgets he owned.
- Who watches: English-speaking tech buyers; at first, other teenagers making tech videos (his account).
- How it earns: Ads, sponsored segments, merch and his own products; no figures disclosed.
- How it grew: No single breakout: each year’s median rose as he moved from tutorials to headphones to phones.
