# Olivia Saad (@oliviasaad3, TikTok): “In case your dad didn’t teach you”: home repairs to 600K

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/olivia-saad/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

She spent a year and a half posting food and family jokes to about 2K views a clip. Small home repairs under one opening line changed that: two caulking tutorials hit 1.8M and 2.4M. In November she said she quit her job.

## The play

Pick one job a parent usually teaches and film yourself doing it in a real room: open with “in case your dad didn’t teach you”, name each tool as you pick it up, and close on the same line.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A clean from-zero turn: 18 months at about 2K a clip, then one opening line on practical home repairs, a 39× higher median and, by her own posts, 5K to almost 100K followers in about 11 weeks. What you can copy is the shape: one line, one real job, every tool named. What you cannot copy is a house full of projects, and the safety duty that comes with electrical tutorials.

- What it takes: Basic home-repair skill and the nerve to film yourself learning in public.
- Startup cost: Hand tools, materials you would buy anyway, a phone and a tripod.
- Time to a verdict: Her run: three or four weeks of mixing how-tos into daily posting, then the series almost every day for two months.
- Window: Practical how-to never goes out of date. The angle (“the person you would not expect, doing the job”) transfers to cars, gardens or bikes.

## Key findings

1. The topic moved the median, not the posting: She posted about two a day from October 2025 at a 1.3K median. The 384 posts from February 10 on have a median of 68.5K, 39 times the 336 before.
2. One line, every repair: Posts carrying “in case your dad didn’t teach you” have a median of 186K since February 10. Everything else she posted in the same months: 42K.
3. The lifestyle posts stayed cold: The six weakest posts after the turn are all from the same week as the caulking videos: a Bible verse, an office joke, hockey night, carrot juice. Each got 3–4K.
