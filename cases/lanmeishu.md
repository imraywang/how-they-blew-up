## The play

Treat a short drama like a TV series: one strong premise (private detective × high-school heartthrob), cinematic widescreen AI visuals, one episode a week, a hook at the end of each, all in one playlist.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

Anyone can use the AI tools; this account wins on premise and taste: a story you can say in one line, with visuals that look like an American drama. It suits someone who can tell a story, has an eye for images and can ship one episode a week.

- What it takes: Writing a serial plot with suspense, and keeping characters and colour tone consistent across AI video shots. No one needs to appear on camera.
- Startup cost: No actors or live shoots; the cost is the AI tools and the production time for 4–8 minutes per episode. The account has not said what it spent.
- Time to a verdict: The first episode tells you. Stop-loss: if EP01 does not pass 1M views, change the premise; do not push on to episode five.
- Window: Douyin and Jimeng were still running AI creation events in October, so a new entrant can still catch the support. We did not count similar accounts, and whether the traffic lasts after the events end is unknown.

## Key findings

1. The first video was the hit: EP01 got 47.0M views and 3.45M likes, taking the account from almost nothing to 680K followers, with no test videos before it.
2. A series, not a one-off short: Four episodes of one story with the same cast, in a playlist titled “自制美高《Shame》” (homemade American high school drama Shame), with one episode a week.
3. Later episodes are fading: EP02 has 9.76M and EP03 6.44M. People tap a series episode only if they watched the one before, and most new viewers stop at EP01.
