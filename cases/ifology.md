# IFOLOGY (@IFOLOGYFILMS, YouTube): Superpowers as one serial: 57.7K subscribers in a month

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-12. https://blewup.co/cases/ifology/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

Second-person superpower cartoons tied into one serial by a villain; on day 7 one reached 995K views. Once videos stretched to 25 minutes the median kept falling, and going back to 10 in November didn’t fix it.

## The play

One classic superpower per video; a second-person voice states the rule and works it out with real numbers, the ending leaves a villain as a hook, and each description links the previous episode, turning dozens of videos into one serial; one or two a day, each cut down into a Short.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

Started the same week as Jeremy Bearimy with the same kind of video, it took another route: classic superpowers, with a villain tying dozens of videos into one serial. Day 7 got 995K, day 8 got 741K; in a month 22 videos passed 100K and subscribers reached 57.7K. Worth learning: the first week’s premises and the ending hook. To avoid: three weeks of longer videos, and having nothing ready after the serial ended.

- What it takes: A production setup that turns out one or two animations a day of ten minutes or more, with narration and side characters’ lines; what tools it uses is not public.
- Startup cost: 54 long videos in a month (plus 10 Shorts and a six-hour compilation); cost per video not public.
- Time to a verdict: 995K on day 7; 46.3K subscribers 21 days after the first upload; 57.7K at 34 days.
- Window: Narrowing: the November median fell to 20K, more channels of the same kind, and no monetisation for mass-produced repetitive content on YouTube.

## At a glance

- What it is: Second-person “What IF” superpower animation, tied into one serial by a villain.
- Who watches: Mainly English-speaking viewers, topics aimed at teenagers (our reading); 20 videos have dubbed audio.
- How it earns: YouTube ads and channel memberships; no public revenue.
- How it took off: First-week classic superpowers: 995K on October 15, 741K on the 16th.

## KPI

- Subscribers: 57.7K (First upload Oct 9; 46.3K by Oct 30) [Fact]
- Videos over 100K: 22 / 54 (3 over 500K; top 995K) [Derived]
- Median views, first 8 → November: 239K → 20K (13 videos, Nov 5 – 11) [Derived]
- Long videos a day: 1.5 (54 in 35 days, plus 10 Shorts) [Derived]

## Key findings

1. Classic superpowers worked here: In October, 23 superpower premises had a median of 204K and the other 10 premises 52K. Jeremy Bearimy, the same kind of channel, saw the opposite: a median of only 53K across seven classic superpowers.
2. A villain and description links make a serial: At 9:10 the hit introduces the group “Project IF”, and at 10:00 the villain appears; descriptions link the previous episode. A 51.5-minute finale on November 4 closes it.
3. Three weeks longer, the median kept falling: First eight videos, median 239K; at 17–22 minutes, 183K; at 23–26 minutes, 60K; back at ten minutes, 20K.
