Half a year of student schedules and hackathon logs got a few hundred views. From January 2025 he replied all day and posted every result: angel checks, a turned-down Disney offer, a hackathon win. Today: 20.8K.

## The play

Reply to people your size every day; post each real result on its own, with the number; tell your origin story in one long thread.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

What you can copy is the method: reply daily to people your size, post each result with its number, tell your story once. What you can't copy is the results. Fits a founder or student who has something new to report every few weeks.

- What it takes: A real result every few weeks, and about an hour a day for replies.
- Startup cost: Time only; no paid tools needed.
- Time to a verdict: Judge it after three months of daily replies: his posts in the sample moved from hundreds to thousands of views in that time.
- Window: "Young founder in SF" is a crowded lane on X; a clear label of your own matters more than the city.

## Key findings

1. Replies came first: In January 2025 his replies jumped from 82 to 685 a month. Over the eight months to 10K he replied about seven times for every main post.
2. Every result got its own post: Angel checks 11,623 views, turning down Disney 15,438, the YC hackathon win 150,408. In the sample, opinions and schedules rank low.
3. The break, then the comeback: He posted 6 main posts in 32 days. Four days after he came back, his comeback post reached 172,299 views; his recap thread three weeks later reached 655,546.
4. His thread mostly checks out: "8 months" holds if you count from January 2025. The reply-guy advice and the 200-views start hold. "Frequency doesn't matter" can't be checked: his 10K month was also his busiest.
