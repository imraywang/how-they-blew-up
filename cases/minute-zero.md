## The play

Pick a variable that can escalate without limit (speed, time, size), use one fixed title formula, and make each film nine minutes, first person, one continuous shot from your front street to the edge of the universe, with a live readout on screen; post a dozen in three weeks and see which variable takes off.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A batch format anyone can copy: one title formula, one variable that escalates forever, nine minutes in one shot, a readout in the corner. It hit 2.34M views on the third video and nearly 20K subscribers in 17 days; it also used the formula up in three weeks, with the last batch at a 12.8K median, then stopped. The lesson is in how it tested for three weeks, not in how it tried to continue.

- What it takes: A production process that gets the physics and astronomy right and turns out nine-minute continuous shots; what tools it uses is not public.
- Startup cost: Fourteen nine-minute films in a month; cost per film is not public.
- Time to a verdict: The third video (day three) hit; 19.4K subscribers in 17 days.
- Window: Narrowing: more channels on the format, “AI slop” in the comments, and YouTube does not pay for mass-produced repetitive content.
