# Sofia Pevidor (@SofiaPevidor, YouTube): Hiding squishies in a supermarket added 4.9M

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/sofia-pevidor/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

For eleven months she posted short wordless skits: three went viral (39.5M at the top), yet subscribers sat near 36K. After a 25-day break she came back speaking Portuguese; five days later the supermarket video hit 53.3M.

## The play

A game the viewer plays along with: hide a food-shaped squishy among real groceries and let them spot it. Talk through it, keep it 45–70 seconds, and let YouTube dub it.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The clearest format switch in this batch: eleven months of skits, three viral hits and no channel, then a talk-through supermarket game that grew 36K to 5.3M subscribers. Copy the shape (a game the viewer plays, spoken and dubbed), not the squishy.

- What it takes: Comfortable talking on camera in public, and able to film in shops.
- Startup cost: A phone and a box of food-shaped squishies; time in shops is the real cost.
- Time to a verdict: About one Short every two days; judge each game on three uploads.
- Window: The squishy craze will pass; the hide-and-find structure works with any object that can pass for something on a shelf.

## At a glance

- What it is: Hide-and-find games in supermarkets and parks, spoken in Portuguese.
- Who watches: Portuguese speakers plus dubbed audiences; geography not public.
- How it earns: Shorts ad revenue only, as far as can be seen.
- How it broke out: Dropped wordless skits for spoken challenges after a 25-day break.

## KPI

- Subscribers: 5.37M (36.4K on August 31) [Fact]
- Median after vs before: 211× (7.75M against 36.8K) [Derived]
- Gained in two months: +4.9M (September and October 2026) [Fact]
- Est. ad revenue to date: $7.6K–38K (at $0.02–0.10 per 1,000 views) [Estimate]

## Key findings

1. Viral is not the same as a channel: A 39.5M skit in November 2025 and a 20.4M one in March 2026 left subscribers at about 36K. In March she gained 0.4 subscribers per 1,000 views; after the switch, 17 to 28.
2. A game the viewer plays: Seven hide-and-find Shorts have a median of 26.1M; the other fifteen since the switch, 3.0M. The top five videos are all games where the viewer searches or compares.
3. Talk, longer, and dubbed: Median length went from 10 seconds to 45, the speech to Portuguese, and all 22 Shorts since carry dubbed audio. Median views went from 36.8K to 7.75M.
