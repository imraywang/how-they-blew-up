## The play

Turn years of notes into short posts; offer a free PDF to anyone who follows and replies with a keyword; post your company's real numbers each month.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

What others can borrow is the format: follow-and-reply-for-a-PDF posts, reposted when they work, and monthly real numbers. What they can't borrow is a $30M company, a growth tool of his own and three years of saved writing. His "90 days" is about 155 by his own milestones.

- What it takes: One genuinely useful document, real numbers to share, and two or three posts a day.
- Startup cost: Time; he also uses his own AI growth tool.
- Time to a verdict: About five months from daily posting to 10K, with June 2026 the fastest month.
- Window: PDF-for-a-reply posts are common on founder X; real numbers behind them are less common.
