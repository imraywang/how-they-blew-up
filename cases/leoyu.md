# Leo Yu (@leoyu.mp4, TikTok): Reading picture books to adults: 1.8M on the second book

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/leoyu/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

His first month, talking about his twenties, reached almost no one. Reading picture books to the camera has since carried him to 240K followers; he says 100K came about three months in.

## The play

Open on your face with one line that calls the viewer out, then cut to the book on a table and read the whole thing. Same opening, a new book every day, books picked from viewers’ requests.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A clean format switch: a month of nothing, then a children’s book on day 36 and a median that rose 48×. What you can copy is the shape: one opening line, one fixed shot, a new book every day. What you cannot check is the follower story, which is his own and published by his employer.

- What it takes: Comfortable reading aloud on camera; the face is on screen for only three seconds.
- Startup cost: A phone over a table and a supply of books; a library card works.
- Time to a verdict: His own run: 30 days of daily posts before the format, then about one a day for three months.
- Window: Nostalgia formats keep working, but this exact one is now visible. Pick a different childhood object rather than another book channel.

## At a glance

- What it is: Children’s picture books read aloud, for adults, at bedtime.
- Who watches: Mostly 18 to 25, by his account.
- How it earns: One publisher sponsorship so far; Creator Rewards if enrolled.
- How it broke out: Switched from talking about his twenties to reading books; the second book did 1.8M.

## KPI

- TikTok followers: 240K (flat for the last 30 days) [Fact]
- Median after vs before: 48× (82.9K against 1.7K) [Derived]
- Days to 100K: 84 (Dec 11 → his Mar 5 thank-you) [Self-reported]
- Est. Creator Rewards: $24K–60K (if enrolled, $0.4–1 per 1,000) [Estimate]

## Key findings

1. The switch, not the streak, moved the median: 28 talking-head posts in the first month had a median of 1.7K. The 154 posts from the first book on have a median of 82.9K, 48 times higher.
2. Same opening line, every book: The Very Hungry Caterpillar (165K), If You Give a Mouse a Cookie (1.8M) and I Want My Hat Back (12.0M) all open on his face and the same caption, “if you’re seeing this video”.
3. Off-format posts went cold: After the turn, 21 posts under 45 seconds (fit checks, life updates) had a median of 10.7K; 130 readings of a minute or more had 109K.
