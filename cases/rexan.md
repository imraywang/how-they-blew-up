## The play

Build a small tool you can show in one image and launch it on X; open with who you are; then post your user counts and bills every week.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

What you can copy is the launch: a tool you can show in one image, a demo post, a launch post, then user counts every week. What you can't copy is a launch that catches. Fits an app maker who ships small tools fast.

- What it takes: A working tool people want to try, and something new to post most days after it lands.
- Startup cost: Your time, plus whatever the app costs to run.
- Time to a verdict: Judge it on each launch post: his went from a median of 68 views to 416K in four months.
- Window: Teen and indie app launches are common on X now; the tool has to be clear from one picture.

## Key findings

1. The product was the post: In September 2024 he posted only 12 main posts, but three about text-behind-image drew 959K views between them. Three months later he says he hit 10K.
2. Bills that show user counts: "Here's how the Supabase bill looks when your app has 200K+ users: $200" reached 218,459 views. It reads as a cost and shows the users.
3. His recaps mostly check out: "Results posts grew me most" and "I haven't posted much" hold. "23K in a year" does not: by his own start points it took 16 to 23 months.
4. Going quiet cost him growth: From his fastest stretch, about 83 followers a day, he fell to about 6 a day in 2026, as main posts fell from 1.6 to 0.5 a day.
