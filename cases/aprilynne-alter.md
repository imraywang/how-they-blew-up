# Aprilynne Alter (@AprilynneAlter, YouTube): She left a 22K channel and rebuilt to 186K

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/aprilynne-alter/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

Her new channel teaches how to make YouTube videos. Twenty videos in, a 12-minute guide to intros reached 1.72M; she then cut to about one long video a month, and says her business made over $290,000 last year.

## The play

Teach the one skill your viewers fail at most (intros, thumbnails, titles), in one long, step-by-step video with a free PDF, and keep a fixed title template so each new guide reads like the next episode.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The proof that a channel can grow on one video a month, if every video is a definitive guide to one skill your viewers need. The catch: she started with an earlier 22K channel’s experience and a following on X, and her income comes from coaching, not views.

- What it takes: Real expertise in the thing you teach, and the patience to make one 20-minute guide a month.
- Startup cost: A home studio, a camera and weeks per video; her program description mentions a team.
- Time to a verdict: About one guide a month; give each title template three videos before changing it.
- Window: Open for any skill people search for. YouTube growth itself is crowded; the template transfers to other crafts.

## At a glance

- What it is: Long, step-by-step guides to making YouTube videos.
- Who watches: Aspiring and small YouTubers.
- How it earns: A 12-week coaching program, sponsors and ads: $290K+ in 2025 (self-reported).
- How it broke out: A 12-minute guide to intros reached 1.72M five months after the restart.

## Key findings

1. One skill per video, one title template: Five “killer … (to blow up your channel)” guides have a median of 498K. Before the first one, her 20 videos had a median of 12.0K.
2. Slow uploads, steady subscribers: After the breakout she posted about once a month, with gaps of up to 145 days, and still added 2,000 to 7,000 subscribers almost every month.
3. Her own journey did not sell: The “can I get monetized in 90 days” diary and studies of mid-sized creators sat at 4.5K–31K. The two MrBeast studies were the exception, at 429K and 539K.
