# YD-Jax (@YD-Jax, YouTube): A wordless food test: 19M views on the third upload

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-06. https://blewup.co/cases/yd-jax/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

Fry a chicken leg for one second, thirty seconds, ten minutes, an hour, and taste each one. The format was already proven on Douyin. This channel moved it to YouTube and never needed a cold start.

## The play

Take a wordless food-test format that already works on Douyin (one second to one hour), reshoot it on a black set with English titles, and keep the template fixed once it hits.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The format is easy to copy: simple shoot, cheap props. What is hard to copy is the performer’s face. It suits someone with an expressive host who can post every day.

- What it takes: One expressive, camera-comfortable host. No script, voice-over or editing skill needed.
- Startup cost: Low. A black backdrop, a pan and the food. The real cost per video is ingredients and shooting time.
- Time to a verdict: Ten videos. The original hit 19M on its third; if ten in a row stay under 100K, stop and compare with the originals.
- Window: The top spot in English is open since the original stopped in May. But at least three Douyin accounts run it, and every month leaves fewer fresh foods and variables.

## At a glance

- What it is: A wordless food test: one ingredient at four settings (1 second to 1 hour), tasted on camera.
- Who watches: Anyone. There is nothing to translate, so it travels across languages.
- How it earns: Shorts ad revenue only, and the channel has not posted since May.
- How it broke out: It skipped discovery by using a format already proven on Douyin.

## KPI

- Followers: 1.01M (+16K in the last 30 days, with no uploads) [Fact]
- Median views, first 10 videos: 3.08M (7 of 10 passed a million) [Fact]
- Third video: 19.1M (posted on day two) [Fact]
- Est. ad revenue to date: $5K–27K (at $0.02–0.10 per 1,000 views) [Estimate]

## Key findings

1. A tested format needs no cold start: The one-second-to-one-hour test is an established genre on Douyin. Moved to YouTube with English titles, it hit 19M on the third upload.
2. Wordless video travels: Only timing calls and reactions, a progress bar across the top, and a black set. Viewers in any language can follow it.
3. It broke when the packaging changed: In May the titles became “#shorts”, the pace doubled and at least one old video was re-posted. The median fell from 900K to 250K, and uploads stopped a week later.
