1. The format changed, not the volume: March 2026 had 120 main posts, January and February 103 each. What changed was the content: breakdowns of other apps' monthly revenue.
2. Revenue breakdowns did about 6× the rest: About 20 March posts with another app's monthly revenue up front had a median of about 45,000 views; the rest about 7,600.

4. The recap leaves out the starting point: "2 weeks later → 5.4K followers" is his total on Mar 15, not a gain. "Only post what i use" sits next to posts ending with a tool he says the promo is from.
