# Ali Abdaal (@aliabdaal, YouTube): A med student’s study-tips video hit 5.5M

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/ali-abdaal/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

He began in his last year of medical school with admissions tips and student vlogs, at a 23K median. After one revision guide he made studying the whole channel, left medicine in 2021 and turned it into a company.

## The play

Teach the thing you are visibly good at, backed by evidence and your own results (“I’m a final-year medical student at Cambridge”), then widen the topic one step at a time: study, productivity, money, life.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The early half is the useful part: ten months of med-school content at a 23K median, then one evidence-based study guide that set the direction for everything after. The later half, a team, courses and a book, is a business built on that audience, and its growth has slowed since 2024.

- What it takes: Real, visible credentials in the topic you teach, and the discipline to post twice a week for years.
- Startup cost: A camera and a desk at the start; a team of about a dozen later.
- Time to a verdict: About two long videos a week from 2017 to 2021.
- Window: Study and productivity are crowded now. The opening still works for anyone with proof in a skill people search for.

## At a glance

- What it is: Evidence-based study, productivity and life advice.
- Who watches: Students and young professionals (our reading of the topics).
- How it earns: His courses and products, sponsors, ads, a book; $4.6M revenue in 2022 by his account.
- How it broke out: An evidence-based revision guide in April 2018 reached 5.49M.

## KPI

- Subscribers: 6.71M (+20–30K a month in 2025–26) [Fact]
- The turn video: 239× (5.49M against a 23K median) [Derived]
- To 1M: ~3 years (Jun 2017 → his Sep 2020 Q&A) [Self-reported]
- Revenue, 2022: $4.6M (his own figure, the whole business) [Self-reported]

## Key findings

1. Expertise on screen, evidence in the script: 79 long videos before the turn had a median of 23K. The evidence-based revision guide reached 5.49M, 239 times that; videos from then to the end of 2019 had a median of 186K.
2. Two a week, then wider topics: 65 videos in 2017, 83 in 2018, 102 in 2020. Topics moved from study to productivity, tech, money and life, and the yearly median rose to 587K by 2023.
3. Since 2024, slower: The long-video median fell to 242K in 2025 and 181K in 2026. Monthly subscriber gains fell from about 100K in early 2024 to 20–30K.
