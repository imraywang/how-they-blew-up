## The play

Film the piano from your own eyes, open every Short on the same small ritual (wind the metronome, lift the lid), play one well-known nostalgic song, and post two or three a day so the algorithm keeps testing you.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

Very copyable if you can already play: no face, one camera, no editing beyond a caption. The breakthrough took a new shot plus a jump to two or three a day; expect weeks at your old numbers before a song lands.

- What it takes: Play popular songs cleanly on a real piano, with no mistakes in a 60–100 second take.
- Startup cost: A piano, a metronome and a phone you can hold or mount at eye level. The real cost is two or three clean recordings a day.
- Time to a verdict: Use the stop-loss: 30 Shorts in one setup at two or three a day; if none passes ten times your old median, change the shot or the opening.
- Window: Piano covers are crowded and every song belongs to someone else. The format can be copied in a day, so the edge is song choice and output.
