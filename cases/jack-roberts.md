# Jack Roberts (@jackroberts____, TikTok): One free tool per video: his TikTok median went 765 to 9.9K

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/jack-roberts/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

Not a cold start: he teaches AI automation and had about 200K YouTube subscribers when he began posting daily on TikTok. With the GitHub link first in every caption, a Claude Code repo reached 1.0M.

## The play

One free AI tool per video, named in the first line by what it does for you; 30–45 seconds of face and screen; the link at the top of the caption, not behind a comment.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A useful counter-example to “bring your audience over”: 200K YouTube subscribers did not make his TikTok work. A year and 128 posts later the median was 765. What moved it was TikTok-native packaging: one free tool per video, a first line about the viewer, the link at the top of the caption. That lifted the median 13×. It is still a mid-sized account that has not reached 100K.

- What it takes: Real expertise in the tools you demo, and a fast edit.
- Startup cost: A face camera, a microphone and screen recording; his edits look produced, and who makes them is not public.
- Time to a verdict: Near daily since April 2026; 11 weeks before the format clicked.
- Window: New AI tools ship daily, so the supply of ideas is endless; so is the competition. The free-and-installable angle is the part to copy.

## At a glance

- What it is: One free AI tool per video, for business owners.
- Who watches: Business owners and builders who want to automate work.
- How it earns: A $97-a-month Skool community; tool referral links.
- How it broke out: Changed the packaging, not the topic: one tool, link first, 30–45 seconds.

## KPI

- TikTok followers: 91K (not yet at 100K) [Fact]
- Median after vs before: 13× (9.9K against 765) [Derived]
- YouTube at the restart: 201K (end of April 2026; 295K today) [Fact]
- Biggest TikTok: 1.0M (Everything Claude Code, Sep 16) [Fact]

## Key findings

1. The YouTube audience did not come with him: 128 TikTok posts before July 10, most of them while his YouTube channel was between 62K and 233K subscribers, had a median of 765 views.
2. The link moved to the top: Since July, posts whose caption opens with the tool’s link have a median of 13.5K; the rest, 7.1K. Comment-for-the-link photo posts: 1.2K.
3. Free tools beat news: The two biggest posts are free GitHub projects: Everything Claude Code (1.0M, 60K saves) and Harvey, a cold-email agent (666K).
