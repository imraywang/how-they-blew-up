## Key findings

1. The second video took off: The first, “man or bear”, got 4,999 views; the next day a world without clothes got 1.3M, 34 times the channel median. The channel’s own pinned comment asks viewers how good the thumbnail and title were.
2. Partly held: Four of the next nine passed 200K: 100 Amazons 572K, seeing who loves you 310K, frozen for 1,000 years 250K, invisible 205K; the other five stayed under 40K.
3. The hit did not convert much: Three days after the 1.3M video the channel had 2,020 subscribers; as the mid-sized hits came out it added 660–1,570 a day and passed 10K in 11 days.
