# Giovana Quintanilha (@gigi_quintanilha, YouTube): A 3,200-subscriber channel posted one skit: 1M in 38 days

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-08. https://blewup.co/cases/giovana-quintanilha/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

A Brazilian creator and two friends film Portuguese skits outdoors on a phone. The first video on the channel today has 35.2M views; five follow-ups held a 4.83M median, and superhero costumes cut views to half a million.

## The play

Give yourself one power the audience understands in a sentence (she sees a prank before it happens), stop the same friend being hurt twice in under a minute, and write the like-and-subscribe ask into the joke.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The premise is easy to borrow: a power, a friend, a small disaster, under a minute. The hard part is a cast of three who can act it out several times a week, and her channel shows how fast it fades once the premise is dropped.

- What it takes: Two or three people who can act broadly on camera, and someone who writes tight 40-second scenes.
- Startup cost: A phone, daylight, a few props. No studio or special effects beyond a sticker or a flame overlay.
- Time to a verdict: One skit every two or three days; judge each premise on five skits and drop it if the median falls below a fifth of your best.
- Window: Short-skit Shorts are crowded in Portuguese and Spanish. A clear premise travels; her superhero costumes did not.

## Key findings

1. One premise, explained in the first line: “I had a terrible vision” tells the viewer the rule in two seconds: she sees a prank coming and stops it. It reached 35.2M views on a channel with 3,200 subscribers.
2. Her follow-ups held, until she changed the premise: Five skits from October 1–17 kept a 4.83M median and added the two biggest subscriber jumps (+97K and +279K in a day). The next five, tricks and superhero costumes, fell to 511K.
3. The ask is part of the joke: Several skits build “like and subscribe” into the scene, including the 16.8M Stop Copying Me! and the 9.09M He Never Knows How to Play. Every Short is also dubbed, 7 to 18 audio tracks.
