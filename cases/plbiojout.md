## The play

Post every real milestone of what you're building, number first; then write long, sectioned comparisons from the two worlds you know.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

What you can copy is the method: number-first milestone posts while you build, and long comparisons from two places you know. What you can't copy is the startup and the two countries. Fits a founder or expat with a real project and a strong point of view.

- What it takes: A project with numbers worth sharing, and a perspective from somewhere your readers aren't.
- Startup cost: Time only; about one post a day.
- Time to a verdict: Judge it over months: his launch posts reached tens of thousands, his first million-view post came seven months later.
- Window: Founder ARR posts are crowded; the comparisons are his own.
