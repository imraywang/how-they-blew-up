## The play

One man, one dog and one small fear at a time: the first beach, the first hot yoga class, the third try at Pilates. Say the context in the first two seconds and let the dog or the fear do the rest.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A head account whose growth came early: nine months of practice on other platforms, then a man-and-dog format that hit on Instagram within three weeks. Followers kept climbing past 1.1M while the median per Reel fell by more than half. Copy the hook discipline (context in two seconds, one small fear), and watch your median, not just your followers.
