# Jay Clouse (@jay, YouTube): Strategist interviews: 100K subscribers in two years

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/jay-clouse/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

Not a cold start: before YouTube he had a five-year newsletter and a business doing over $300K a year, on his own monthly figures. A year of business interviews sat at a 5.4K median; after the switch, one reached 5M.

## The play

Film the podcast you already make, then pick guests and topics for the YouTube audience rather than for your existing listeners: he switched to the people behind big YouTube channels, packaged each one as “Meet the…”, and planned the title before the interview.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A useful case for anyone who already has an audience elsewhere. His podcast filmed as-is did little on YouTube; picking guests and topics for YouTube viewers is what moved it. It took about two years to 100K with an editor and a business behind him, and the channel still runs at a loss.

- What it takes: Access to guests your target viewers want to hear, and the habit of writing the title before you record.
- Startup cost: An editor from the start, two cameras, and money to spare: he says the channel has lost money most years.
- Time to a verdict: About one long interview a week at first, then two a month.
- Window: Interviews about growing on YouTube are crowded now. The method (guests chosen for the platform’s audience, packaged first) carries over to other niches.

## At a glance

- What it is: Long interviews with the people behind successful creators.
- Who watches: People building a business on YouTube, newsletters and podcasts (our reading of the topics).
- How it earns: Mostly his paid community; also sponsors, courses and ads. The channel itself loses money, by his account.
- How it broke out: A switch to YouTube-strategy guests in June 2023, then a 5.02M Shorts interview in October.

## KPI

- Subscribers: 145K (+0–2K a month in 2026) [Fact]
- After the guest switch: 26× (138.6K median vs 5.4K before) [Derived]
- To 100K: ~2 years (“a little over 2 years”, Jul 2022 → Aug 2024) [Self-reported]
- The Lab, 2025: $500K/yr (recurring revenue, his own figure) [Self-reported]

## Key findings

1. The podcast, filmed, didn’t travel: 42 long interviews from July 2022 to May 2023 had a median of 5,351 views. He had about 3,000 subscribers in January 2023, six months in, despite a newsletter of roughly 16K.
2. Guests for the YouTube audience: From June 2023 he interviewed the strategists, writers and creators behind big YouTube channels. Those six videos had a median of 138.6K; the next, with Jenny Hoyos, reached 5.02M. He went from 20K to 53.7K subscribers in about five weeks.
3. 100K, then a plateau: He passed 100K at the end of August 2024. In 2025 he posted fewer videos, the median fell to 7.9K, and growth slowed to 0–4K a month.
