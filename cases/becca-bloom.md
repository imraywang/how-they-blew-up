# Becca Bloom (@beccaxbloom, TikTok): One get-to-know-me video: 21.9M views on day nine

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/becca-bloom/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

She started with no audience, posting up to four luxury hauls and outfits a day. Her intro had the Bay Area, a fiancé in tech and “extremely successful” parents. Now 6.3M, with a talent agency; all 552 videos still up.

## The play

Introduce yourself early and plainly, then turn the life people are curious about into a few repeatable formats: hauls, “plate my breakfast with me”, a wedding, answered requests.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A head account built in weeks, and almost none of it is luck you can schedule: a rare life on camera, a plain self-introduction on day nine, then a handful of recurring formats. What transfers is the structure, an early introduction and fixed series titles. What does not is the subject. Whether the persona is staged is our open question, not a finding.

- What it takes: A life people are curious about, and the ease to talk about it to a phone.
- Startup cost: The objects on screen; the filming itself is a phone at home.
- Time to a verdict: Up to four a day in her first weeks; about four a week now.
- Window: RichTok had its moment in 2025; her median has been falling through 2026. The early-introduction move is timeless.

## At a glance

- What it is: Luxury everyday life: hauls, jewellery, breakfasts, a wedding.
- Who watches: Not published by TikTok; we did not measure it.
- How it earns: Brand partnerships through a talent agency.
- How it broke out: A plain self-introduction on day nine: 21.9M.

## KPI

- TikTok followers: 6.3M (from zero on Jan 7, 2025) [Fact]
- Days to 100K: 40 (her “100k … in a month?” post) [Self-reported]
- Day-nine intro video: 21.9M (22× her first 21 videos’ median) [Derived]
- Labelled brand posts: 16 (median 7.0M views) [Fact]

## Key findings

1. She introduced herself on day nine: Her 23rd video, “Hi! Get to know me”, is a 79-second front-camera talk with no edit. It has 21.9M views, 22 times the median of her first 21 videos.
2. Series titles carried the long run: “Plate my breakfast with me” ran 21 times from March 2025 to October 2026 at a median of 9.8M; 103 of her 552 videos passed 10M.
3. The median is falling: From 11.7M in May 2026 to 4.8M in August and 2.5M in October, while she kept posting 15–22 a month.
