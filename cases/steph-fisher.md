# Steph Fisher (@stephfi___, Instagram): An engineer mom filmed her 4:30 a.m. mornings: one hit 8.6M

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-09. https://blewup.co/cases/steph-fisher/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

A Maryland civil engineer and mother of four, she says she started from zero followers. The hit was her husband narrating “my wife's 5-to-9”; three more passed 4M in 18 days. By her own posts: $100K in 14 months.

## The play

One recognizable promise, filmed every day: a mother of four who works full time and still gets up at 4:30 to train. Timestamps on screen, the real house, no studio.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A real but hard-to-repeat start: four viral morning-routine Reels in the second month of a brand-new account, then 20 months of daily vlogs that held a 47K median and doubled the followers. The repeatable part is the daily habit and the honest income posts; the viral month was luck we cannot model.

- What it takes: A daily life people find hard to believe, and comfort filming your family.
- Startup cost: A phone and CapCut. The cost is getting up at 4:30 and posting every day.
- Time to a verdict: Her break came in month two; most of the growth after it took a year of daily posts.
- Window: The morning-routine genre is crowded. A specific identity (job plus family size) is what made hers stand out.

## At a glance

- What it is: Daily morning and family vlogs from a working mother of four, with timestamps.
- Who watches: Mothers, mostly in the US.
- How it earns: Brand deals through a manager, affiliate links, a $147 course (her figures: $100K in 14 months).
- How it broke out: Her husband narrated her 4:30 a.m. routine; four Reels passed 4M within 18 days.

## KPI

- Followers: 202K (0 in February 2025 (her posts)) [Fact]
- Views from four Reels: 49M (Mar 25 – Apr 11, 2025; half of all her views) [Derived]
- Median since May 2025: 47.6K (Same in 2025 and 2026 (47.5K)) [Fact]
- Revenue, first 14 months: $100K (Her own post, May 2026) [Self-reported]

## Key findings

1. Her husband narrated the first hit: 26 days after her first post, her husband told her morning from his side of the bed: 8.6M views. She says one Reel took her from under 100 followers to 20,000+ almost overnight; this was her first to pass 1M, so it is most likely the one.
2. Four hits in 18 days, then a flat median: The four Reels between March 25 and April 11, 2025 hold 49M views, half her total. Her median was 60K that month and has been about 47K ever since.
3. The income posts became content too: 106 Reels about money and creating have a 50K median, the same as her vlogs. She says the course made over $60,000 in its first 8 days.
