## The play

Go all in for a month at 10–20 main posts a day plus more replies; shout out accounts your size; give away your study notes as a site; write about your own student and job-hunting life.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

## Key findings

1. Volume was the strategy: 483 main posts in the 30 days from Oct 15, 2025, and about 63 replies a day in his first 90 days. His fastest follower stretch came right after.
2. His own life travelled furthest: Of the 5 posts in our sample past 20K views, two are job-hunt complaints and one is an auto-rickshaw story. The top post of his first two weeks is a campus story.
3. A free site gave people a reason to follow: "Many people have been asking for… my interview prep notes" got 209 bookmarks; the InterviewPrep launch reached 68,158 views.
4. A plateau at 5,000: From Mar 31 to Jul 5, 2026 he gained about 1,000, and posted that he had "reached 5,500 followers for the 6th time".
