# Dan Koe (@thedankoe, X): Dan Koe cut X posts by three-quarters; median views rose 6×

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-12. https://blewup.co/cases/dankoe/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

He began in 2019 at 149 followers (self-reported) with daily self-improvement one-liners. In January 2026 he switched to long X articles; one reached 258M views. He also has 1.45M on YouTube and a software startup.

## The play

Years of short daily posts on one theme built the base; in 2026 he posted far less and put the effort into long, practical articles, each one a full method with exercises.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

A head account with six years of short daily posts behind it. What is worth copying is the 2026 switch: far fewer posts, each long article a full method, timed to when X was pushing articles. What can't be copied is the base: an audience in the hundreds of thousands, 1.45M on YouTube, and years of the same ideas to draw from.

- What it takes: Years of posting on one theme, and enough depth to write a 5,000-word method on it.
- Startup cost: Time: each article is days of writing; he also has an editor and an app team.
- Time to a verdict: His first 10K took about 8 months (calc); the 258M article came six years in.
- Window: Long self-improvement articles on X are crowded now; a long method in a narrower field still has room.

## Key findings

1. Six years of short posts first: 1,432 main posts in 2020 and 1,881 in 2021, most of them one idea in a few lines. That built the audience the articles later reached.
2. One article, 258M views: How to fix your entire life in 1 day: 258,434,052 views and 1,012,462 bookmarks, 3,280 times the median of his posts since late 2024.
3. Fewer posts, higher median: About 44 main posts a month before the article, about 11 after; the median went from 70,317 to 422,799.
4. His early numbers check out: "Missed 1 day of tweeting" in three years: 21 days without a main post. His "first year: 10,000" undersells it: he was near 10K at about 8 months.
