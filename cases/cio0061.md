# Cihat Cihangir Erduran (@cio0061, Instagram): A truck driver’s 418M-view Reel brought only 300K followers

Report v1, data as of 2026-11-06. https://blewup.co/cases/cio0061/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo

In his cab he lip-syncs Black Sea folk songs and pulls faces at his phone. Over two years, 132 Reels have 700M views in total, and 60% of them come from a single one. The hit brought sponsors, but not followers to match.

## The play

Fix one workplace setting (the truck cab) and one persona; in 10–20 seconds each video does one thing: a facial reaction to a local folk song.

## Can you copy it? [Our read]

The format is light: one phone and one fixed setting. The hard part is a real work setting plus songs everyone local can sing. It suits someone who already has a fixed workplace and can post steadily for a long time.

- What it takes: A real place you are in every day, and being relaxed enough on camera to sing along and pull faces. No editing or voice-over needed.
- Startup cost: Low. A phone in a fixed spot, one take, 10–20 seconds per video. The main cost is time.
- Time to a verdict: One month. Post one a day, and after a month check whether the median is above 500K.
- Window: Hard to say. We found no data on similar accounts, and the format is tied to a local language, so each language market has its own window.

## At a glance

- What it is: 10–20 second clips of a Turkish truck driver lip-syncing folk songs and making faces in his cab.
- Who watches: Turkish viewers, especially drivers and people from the Black Sea region.
- How it earns: Sponsorships: a barbershop, a truck air conditioner, a logistics app; signed with an agency.
- How it broke out: Two years at around a million views a video, then a 10-second good-morning lip-sync reached 418M.

## Key findings

1. Not overnight fame, but two years at around a million: The median was 990K in 2024 and 1.28M in 2025. Before the 418M video he had already posted 99, and 4 of them passed 10M.
2. One hit carries 60% of the views: The 6-21 Reel, 10 seconds long, has 418M views, 60% of the total, while followers are only around 300K.
3. Sponsors came right after the hit: From late July he took deals with a barbershop, a truck air conditioner and a logistics app, and the bio changed to point to the agency @echout.media. The sponsored posts themselves get only 100–360K views.
